# Privacy Policy for Jargon

*Last updated: July 24, 2026*

Jargon is an offline reader for the Jargon File. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## Data collection

Jargon collects no personal information. No account is required or offered.

## Local storage

Your favorited entries are stored on your device only, using Android's local app storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

## Network access

Jargon requests no permissions and makes no network requests. The dictionary content ships inside the app itself.

## Third parties

Jargon contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs.

## Contact

If you have any questions, contact: jargon@hackerapps.com
